Postal data: Japan Post, 2026-10-30 UTF-8 one-record-per-line postcode dataset.
Japan Post states it makes no copyright claim to the postcode data and allows redistribution.
Source: https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/search/zipcode/download/utf-zip.html
Terms: https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/search/zipcode/download/utf-readme.html
No external postcode lookup API is used by this version.
The signed kintone container uses RSA/PKCS#1 v1.5 with SHA-1 as required by the established kintone format.
